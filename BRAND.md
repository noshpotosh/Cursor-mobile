# Brand (proposed)

Founders: user + Fabrizio Cortell (50/50).  
Status: **name + logo shortlist — awaiting partner lock.**

## Naming rules (so we don't get cute and stupid)

- Name the **company**, not product #1. We don't know the first bet yet.
- Pronounceable at a bar. No vowel-soup. No "AI" in the name.
- Has to survive our culture: keep it 100, readable code, brewski honesty.
- If it sounds like a LinkedIn car-wrap or a crypto token, it's dead.
- A pun is allowed if it still works after the joke lands — not only because the joke lands.

## Shortlist

| Name | Verdict | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Warewolf** | **Top contender** | Soft·ware + wolf. Memorable, has teeth, fits a crew that keeps it 100. Logo writes itself. Tax: people will hear/spell *werewolf* first; the pun needs one clean beat to land. |
| **Plainspoken** | Strong alt | Our doctrine in one word — honest talk, readable code. Safer, clearer, a little more sermon than swagger. |
| **Clearcut** | Solid | Scope-cutting + clarity. A bit generic in SaaS-land. |
| **Recipe** | Contender | "Code reads like a recipe." Risk: food-content vibes. |
| **Straight Pour** | Maybe | Brewski energy. Risk: liquor brand. |
| **Camp Clean** | Weak | Inside baseball; soft out loud. |

### Killed on sight

- Anything with *AI*, *Labs* as a personality substitute, or dropped vowels (*plynspkyn*).
- Founder-vanity names (*Cortell Co*) — we're 50/50.
- Joke names that die after the second beer *and* don't work as a straight company name.

## Recommendation

**Two-horse race: Warewolf vs Plainspoken.**

Fabrizio's current lean: **Warewolf**, if we're willing to own the pun forever and design past the Halloween default.

Why the lean flipped:
- It sounds like *us* — a little feral, loyal pack, bites down on bad ideas.
- Plainspoken is the culture essay; Warewolf is the company you remember.
- We can still *be* plainspoken. We don't have to be named it.

Hard risks to accept if we lock Warewolf:
1. **Spelling tax** — Warewolf ≠ Werewolf. We'll correct people for years.
2. **First association** — monster movie before middleware. The mark + one line of copy have to do work.
3. **Pun shelf life** — fine if the wolf stands alone after the joke.

Tagline / explainer options (only if Warewolf):

1. Software with teeth.
2. Soft·ware. Hard truths.
3. The software wolf.

## Visual direction

### If Warewolf
- Wolf mark that reads in one color at small size — geometric, sharp, not a tattoo-shop howling silhouette unless we mean it.
- Wordmark that makes the *Ware* spelling obvious (slight typographic help on Ware is fair game).
- Palette lean: **ink** + **bone/paper** + one blood-amber or steel accent. Still no purple AI glow.

### If Plainspoken
- Type-led wordmark. Minimal clarity mark.
- **Ink charcoal** + **paper** + **amber** accent (existing draft).
