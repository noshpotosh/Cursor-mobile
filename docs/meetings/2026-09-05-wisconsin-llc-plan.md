# Meeting — Wisconsin LLC plan for Warewolf

**Date:** 2026-09-05  
**Present:** User + Fabrizio  
**Goal:** Register **Warewolf LLC** in Wisconsin with minimum time/braincycles.

## Recommended path (DIY, online)

File **Articles of Organization (Form 502)** online with WI DFI for **$130**. Skip paper ($170). Skip paid formation services unless you want to pay someone to click buttons for you.

## Super simple steps

1. **Check the name** — Search that `Warewolf LLC` (or `Warewolf, LLC`) is free.  
   https://apps.dfi.wi.gov/apps/corpsearch/Search.aspx

2. **Pick a registered agent** — Usually you. Needs a **Wisconsin street address** (no P.O. box) + email. You’re fine if you live/work in WI.

3. **File the LLC online ($130)** — Articles of Organization, Form 502.  
   Start here: https://dfi.wi.gov/Pages/BusinessServices/BusinessEntities/FileOnline.aspx  
   Direct formation flow: https://apps.dfi.wi.gov/apps/CorpFormation/directions.aspx?type=12  
   Optional one-stop (LLC + tax stuff): https://onestop.wi.gov/OpenMyBusiness (~$131 with portal fee)

4. **Get a free EIN from the IRS** — Needed for a bank account. Takes minutes.  
   https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/apply-for-an-employer-identification-number-ein-online

5. **Open a business bank account** — Bring Articles + EIN. Keep hot-boy money out of personal checking.

6. **Slap together a one-page operating agreement** — Not required by WI for a solo LLC, still smart. Template later if you want; don’t block filing on this.

7. **Calendar the annual report** — WI wants one around your formation anniversary quarter. Don’t forget or they get spicy.

## Skip for now (unless something forces it)

- FinCEN BOI for a domestic US LLC — currently **not required** for US companies ([FinCEN alert](https://www.fincen.gov/))
- Sales tax / extra licenses — only when you actually sell taxable stuff or need a special license
- Paying LegalZoom/Incfile — optional convenience tax
