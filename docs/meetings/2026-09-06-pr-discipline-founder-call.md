# Meeting — PR discipline & founder merge authority

**Date:** 2026-09-06  
**Who:** Nosh (founder), Fabrizio Cortell (recording / enforcing)  
**Topic:** Stop sloppy multi-concern PRs; Nosh alone is final say

## What was said

Nosh called out that PRs and merges were getting sloppy — multiple
features/meetings in one PR, weak branching, hard to revert. He
wants **final say on merges** — himself, not Fabrizio — and wants
decisions / meetings / features separated into well-described PRs.

## Call

**Nosh decided.** Locked as **ADR 006** (Deciders: Nosh only).
Fabrizio documents and enforces scoped PRs; he is not a Decider
and does not merge. Doctrine in `docs/pr-discipline.md`. Always-on
agent rule added. Culture updated.
