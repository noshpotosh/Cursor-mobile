# ADR 001 — Company name: Warewolf

**Status:** Locked  
**Date:** 2026-09-05  
**Deciders:** User + Fabrizio Cortell

## Decision

The company is **Warewolf** (soft·ware + wolf). Never *Werewolf* in official materials.

## Why

- Memorable pack energy; fits how we actually work
- Pun is intentional and brandable
- Beats safer alt **Plainspoken** on swagger without abandoning plainspoken *behavior*

## Spelling defense (required)

- Wordmark: `Ware` monospace/amber · `wolf` bold/bone
- Mark: wolf from software syntax (`<>`, `{}`, caret) — not a full-moon tattoo wolf
- Icon: same wolf + `WARE`
- One-liner when needed: "software with teeth"

## Consequences

- Products get their own names; company stays Warewolf
- Plainspoken logo archived under `brand/plainspoken-logo.png` (not primary)
- Visual system lives in `docs/brand.md`
